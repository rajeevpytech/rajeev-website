## Worldwide remote availability and local distance

Google's local ranking guidance identifies relevance, distance and prominence as major local-result factors. Serving clients remotely in another country does not remove the distance component or establish a nearby physical business. A candidate service page may truthfully describe remote availability, but it must not imply an unverified local office. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)

Google's spam policies describe doorway abuse and scaled content abuse, including substantially similar location-targeted pages and large amounts of unoriginal content created mainly for search manipulation. Review useful differentiation before publication; a large URL count is not a ranking strategy or a guarantee. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
