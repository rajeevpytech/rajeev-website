## Market work within the same 13-week programme

- Days 1–30: preserve existing paths and read the India/UK/US evidence; verify the remote article publicly after server deployment. Prepare new guides for broad remote-business use rather than country-swapped copies.
- Days 31–60: compare UAE, Canada, Australia and Ireland page/query evidence, enquiries and actual operational needs. Record recommendations for existing pages without editing them.
- Days 61–90: inspect Singapore/Germany evidence and compare earlier groups on equivalent periods. Preserve existing language content; choose future new work from useful gaps, not country count.

Off-page evidence should identify real work and accurate business identity. Remote availability does not establish an office, local telephone number or customer-facing premises in any target country. No profile or review should assert a local presence that has not been established.
